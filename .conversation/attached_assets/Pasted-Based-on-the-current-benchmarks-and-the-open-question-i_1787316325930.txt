Based on the current benchmarks and the open question in your Replit chat, here's my take on both decisions you're facing.

## Data sourcing: go with the manual starter set

For a benchmark like this, start with a **small manually-approved starter set** (10-15 calls) rather than an automated de-identification pipeline. Building an automatic PII-scrubbing pipeline for VINs, RO numbers, names, and addresses is itself a project — get it wrong once and you've leaked real customer data to six external vendors. Once your scoring script and gold transcripts prove out on the small set, scale the same manual review process to the full 50-100 calls.

## Provider shortlist: keep all six for round 1, but expect a shakeout

Two things jump out for your specific case:

- **Deepgram vs AssemblyAI is a genuine toss-up on paper** — Deepgram's own benchmark favors Deepgram, an independent benchmark favors AssemblyAI, and neither of those tests your actual alphanumeric-heavy, noisy-shop-floor call data. This is exactly why your planned harness matters more than any published number — you can't trust vendor-reported WER for your use case. [novascribe](https://novascribe.ai/how-accurate-is-assemblyai)
- **OpenAI Whisper/gpt-4o-transcribe is the weakest streaming candidate** on paper (highest WER, not built for real-time), so it's a reasonable one to drop early if VAPI support or time is tight, unless you want it as a cheap fallback for non-latency-sensitive verticals. [speechmatics](https://www.speechmatics.com/company/articles-and-news/best-speech-to-text-ai-guide-apis-platforms-and-services-compared)

All six are supported as VAPI transcriber options, so provider integration isn't a blocker for testing any of them. [vapi](https://vapi.ai/blog/azure-alternatives)
